MEASUREMENT EXTENSION 006 — EXPERIMENTAL ONLY

The cohort and all new questionnaire-range/sleep measurements are
simulated. No real questionnaire responses or astronaut records are
included. New score fields are additional noisy views of current
simulator latent states; observed gains can reflect these assumptions.
They are not evidence of real KSS/Samn–Perelli instrument benefit.

RF26 is the fixed control. Three RF extensions are compared inside
84 TRAIN people using three participant-disjoint folds. Mean macro
F1 rounded to six decimals selects the extension; ties prefer fewer
features, then name. XGB26 versus XGB33 is a separate paired check.
Every imputer is fitted on fitting observations only. Missing score
values are not coded as zero. Identifiers, labels, internal latent
states and future timestamps never enter predictors.

All choices/artifacts are frozen before validation and fresh test.
Both holdouts are evaluated once; neither triggers refitting or
reselection. Participant bootstrap intervals are conditional on a
single fixed synthetic scenario, not population clinical evidence.
Scores must not be compared directly with the old-cohort 0.5273.

The original model/API remains unchanged regardless of results.
