2026-11-14

Conclusion
The proposed comparison is appropriate for the user's request: fit the fixed
experimental exclusion candidate on the original training partition and compare
it with the original control on the same validation cohort that produced 0.5273.
This is a further development comparison on reused validation, not an independent
test or proof that the excluded records are erroneous. No promotion is implied.

   participant-disjoint partitions. RF parameters remain 200 trees, depth 14,
   minimum leaf 8, balanced class weights, seed 20261114, and n_jobs 2.
2. Estimate medians and MAD on the full 5,040-row TRAIN partition only, using
   precisely the four baseline-relative features in the frozen noise_004 plan.
   Flag the union of absolute robust z > 4.5, with scale 1.4826 * MAD; skip any
   feature with zero MAD. No target, event annotation or validation observation
   may influence thresholds, exclusions or candidate parameters.
3. Exclude flagged rows from this candidate's fitting view only. The earlier
   descriptive audit suggests 254 excluded and 4,786 retained; recompute the
   rule to verify those counts, rather than construct a mask from annotations.
4. Preserve every retained row's original target, baseline and past-only feature
   values. Dropping a fitting observation does not mean removing it from the
   measurement stream. Never shift targets or recompute rolling windows over
   the filtered row gaps, which would change the forecast horizon and semantics.
5. Fit preprocessing on each candidate's fitting view. Balanced class weights
   naturally recompute on the retained class distribution for the exclusion
   candidate. Record class counts and this consequence of the fixed recipe.
6. Score all 1,080 original validation rows from all 18 validation participants.
   Do not filter, clip, relabel or otherwise simplify the evaluation cohort.
   The filtered candidate remains able to predict unusual observations.
7. Refit the unchanged full-TRAIN control or use the official saved control,
   and verify its predictions against the official model and saved historical

8. Include current-fatigue persistence. Report all per-class precision/recall/F1,

9. Freeze the new plan and script hashes before fitting or examining new candidate

10. Preserve the original dataset and official artifact. Any saved candidate
    should be explicitly experimental and separate from the production bundle.
    Do not open or rescore the final-test observation file.

Interpretation safeguards
The earlier exclusion experiment produced a small positive grouped-CV gain
(0.00356) and positive forward-time changes, but failed its prespecified +0.01
mean-gain criterion. Its participant-bootstrap interval included zero. A later
validation increase does not retrospectively make that gate pass.

Validation has already supported algorithm and feature comparisons. A new score
on it is directly comparable with 0.5273 for this development exercise, but it is
not fresh independent confirmation. Paired whole-participant bootstrap intervals
should resample the same 18 participants for both candidates; describe them as
descriptive uncertainty on reused synthetic validation. They do not account for
all earlier selection, simulator mismatch or real-world deployment uncertainty.

All 254 full-TRAIN descriptive flags were deliberately injected physiological
events. Filtering may reduce their influence on the separate fatigue forecast,
but it does not establish corruption. Keep them in the source data and in the
anomaly-detection task. More difficult or threshold-near fatigue outcomes must
not be deleted or relabeled simply to improve the score.

No implementation-specific approval or permission barrier is introduced here.
Proceed with the user's authorized comparison under this fixed design.
