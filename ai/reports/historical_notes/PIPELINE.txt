  ('imputer', SimpleImputer(strategy='median')),
  ('model', RandomForestClassifier(
      n_estimators=200, max_depth=14, min_samples_leaf=8,
      class_weight='balanced', random_state=20261114, n_jobs=2
  ))
])

The input feature contract is astra_shield_ml/features.py.

The serving code is astra_shield_ml/service.py and api.py.

SUPPORTING COMPONENTS

Fatigue score and next-day reaction time use separate histogram-gradient-boosting
regressors. Current physiology uses Isolation Forest with HR/RMSSD and baseline
features. Current generated-journal analysis uses a training-fitted TF-IDF plus
logistic classifier. Journal classifications are displayed separately rather
than being silently fed into the numeric fatigue classifier.

CHRONOLOGY OF EDA VS FEATURE ENGINEERING

Feature engineering and source quality/causality validation were already present
before model fitting. The standalone statistical EDA report and its figures were
produced after initial model training, when requested. That report was not a
prerequisite that had secretly been completed earlier. It found no failed defined
consistency checks, and did not change the frozen features, models or thresholds.

For the next development experiment, do training-only EDA before candidate
fitting. If its findings prompt changes, use training/validation for development
and a genuinely new untouched test cohort for new performance claims. Do not
relabel the already evaluated cohort as unseen or train until its score improves.

The current EDA is reports/eda/EDA_REPORT.txt and its accompanying charts/CSVs.
All data and metrics are synthetic demonstration results, not medical validation.
